## Custom pagination

- Global pagination: `LimitOffsetPagination` (PAGE_SIZE=2) from `config/settings.py`.
  Used automatically by **viewsets** and **generic views** — currently in
  `api_viewset` (DoctorsViewSet) and `blog_nexted_serializer` (Blog/Comment generics).
- Custom pagination: `CustomPagination` in `config/paginations.py` (PageNumberPagination,
  page_size=2, page_query_param='page_num', wrapped links/count/results response).
  Used only with `APIView`, instantiated manually — currently in
  `employees/views.py` (EmployeeList) and `student_api/views.py`.
